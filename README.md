## 📖 Overview

Car Rental is built to bridge the gap between people who need a reliable ride and fleet management. Instead of bloated legacy software, it delivers a sleek single-page app (SPA) experience with real-time availability, clear pricing breakdowns, and an intuitive owner portal.

Whether a customer is looking for a daily commuter or a weekend getaway vehicle, Drive Fleet makes the booking pipeline quick and transparent.

---

## 🔥 Key Features

### 👤 Customer Experience
* **Dynamic Search & Filtering:** Filter vehicles by brand, category, seating capacity, and availability.
* **Instant Reservation Flow:** Simple multi-step booking process with immediate date-range validation.
* **Personal Dashboard:** Track active trips, review past rental history, and view booking statuses.

### 🔑 Admin & Fleet Owner Portal
* **Inventory Management:** Full CRUD capabilities—add new vehicles with custom specs and image URLs.
* **Booking Control Center:** Monitor current reservations, update status (Pending, Confirmed, Completed), and review fleet occupancy.
* **Protected Routes:** JWT-backed authorization to safeguard admin endpoints and user data.
